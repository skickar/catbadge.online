Community BINs

Badge images built and shared by the community. Anyone can submit one — and anyone can flash them here, over Web Serial, the same way as the main catalog.

!! these images are UNTESTED and UNVERIFIED
Unlike the main firmware catalog — every image there is hardware-verified — nothing on this page has been tested or vetted by Retia. A community image may not boot, may behave unexpectedly, may be transmit- or attack-capable, or may leave the badge needing manual recovery (hold GPIO_0, tap RESET). Flash these only on a badge you own, and only if you accept that risk. The site will ask you to confirm before every community flash. If one bricks the boot, recover and flash a known-good image from the main catalog.

Submit your own image

Built something for the badge? Share it. Submissions come in by pull request to the site's repo — you add your merged factory.bin plus one JSON entry, a maintainer looks it over, and it shows up here (still labelled untested).

// what you provide
  • Project name
  • Link to source (public repo)
  • What it does — a sentence or two
  • A merged factory.bin for the ESP32-S3 badge, flashable at 0x0
// how it lands
  1. Open the proposal form, or go straight to a PR.
  2. Add the .bin under community/firmware/ + an entry in community/community.json.
  3. A maintainer validates it and merges — it appears here on the next build.
